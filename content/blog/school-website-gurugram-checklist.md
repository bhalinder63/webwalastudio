Parents in Gurugram researching a school for their child almost always start online — long before a phone call or a campus visit. If your school's website is a static page from years ago, or doesn't exist at all beyond a directory listing, you're losing consideration before you're even in the running. Here's what an effective school website actually needs.

## 1. A clear, fast admissions path

The single most important page on a school website is the one that answers "how do I apply?" — and it needs to answer it in one click from the homepage, not buried three menus deep. At minimum this means:

- An admissions overview: age/grade eligibility, key dates, required documents.
- A downloadable or fillable admission form.
- A direct way to ask a question — WhatsApp or a contact form that actually reaches someone.

## 2. A notice board that staff can actually update

Most school websites we've seen fail not because of bad design, but because the "Notices" section hasn't been updated in eight months — because whoever built it made updating it require a developer. A notice board should be simple enough for office staff to manage without technical help, so it stays current.

## 3. Fee information and online collection

Parents specifically search for fee structure before enrolling — hiding this information doesn't protect you, it just pushes parents to call and ask, which is slower for everyone. Where possible, online fee collection removes a genuine operational headache during admission season.

## 4. A real gallery, not four stock photos

Photos of your actual campus, classrooms, and events do more to build trust than any paragraph of text. This is one of the highest-leverage, lowest-effort improvements a school website can make.

## 5. A parent portal, even a simple one

It doesn't need to be a full LMS — even a simple portal for circulars, event calendars, and basic academic updates meaningfully reduces the "did you see the notice about—" phone calls to the office.

## Gurugram-specific considerations

Gurugram parents are comparing schools against a genuinely competitive set — corporate-adjacent private schools, international curricula, and long-established institutions all in the same search results. A dated or slow website reads as a signal about the school itself, fair or not. Being explicit about location (which sector, proximity to key roads, transport availability) also helps local search — both for parents searching generically and for Google surfacing your school for "school near me" queries in Gurugram.
