If you've searched for "website cost India" and landed on quotes ranging from ₹3,000 to ₹3,00,000, you're not imagining things — the range really is that wide, and almost none of it is explained. Here's what actually drives the price, and what you should expect to pay for a genuinely professional business website in 2026.

## The three things that actually move the price

**Page count.** A 5-page site (home, about, services, gallery, contact) costs less to build than a 15-page site with dedicated pages per service or location — not because of "more work" in some vague sense, but because more pages means more content to structure, more copy to write or edit, and more testing across devices.

**Functionality beyond static pages.** A site that just displays information costs less than one that needs booking forms, payment processing, inventory tracking, or a parent/patient portal. Each of these is a real feature with its own build and testing time, not a checkbox.

**E-commerce.** Selling online — a real product catalog, cart, and payment gateway — is its own category of cost, separate from a marketing site with a "buy now that redirects to WhatsApp" workaround.

## What's *not* included in most quotes — and should be

Two costs sit outside almost every web design quote, including ours, and it's worth knowing about them upfront rather than being surprised:

- **Domain name** — roughly ₹800–1,500/year.
- **Hosting** — roughly ₹3,000–6,000/year.

If a quote you've received bundles these into a suspiciously low one-time number, ask what happens in year two — that's usually where the real cost shows up.

## Why "cheaper" quotes are often more expensive later

A ₹3,000 website is rarely a ₹3,000 website — it's usually a template with no real customization, no mobile testing, and no maintenance window, which means the first bug or the first design change costs you again, often at a worse rate than if it had been priced properly from the start. The honest way to compare quotes isn't the sticker price — it's price divided by (pages + features + post-launch support included).
